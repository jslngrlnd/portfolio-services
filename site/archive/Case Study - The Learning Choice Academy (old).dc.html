The brief

The Learning Choice Academy is a San Diego public charter home school. Its site had to feel engaging and safe, explain the charter home school experience, and motivate students.

The school has four resource centers with different curriculum. The challenge was presenting them as one school while showing what makes each one unique. Students use the site to find curriculum and connect with teachers, parents use it for events and community, and it also has to drive enrollment.

Research

As Design Director I put together a small team of a designer and a developer. I led research and UX, art directed the design, and designed the homepage.

We interviewed 12 stakeholders, including staff and teachers, and collected 42 survey responses from parents, teachers and students. I wrote and presented a summary of the findings to get everyone on the same page.

Three people to design for

The research pointed to three main user types: the first-time parent looking for information, the weekly parent looking for student resources, and the student looking for upcoming testing dates.

From these we built personas, user journeys and the site architecture.

Architecture and brand

We reorganized the site around wayfinding for curriculum and programs, added a section calling out the four locations, and paired clear paths to each resource center with upcoming events.

Family stories went up front to build trust, and the enrollment button was made clear and visible on every page. Once the UX was settled we developed a visual language, including a redesigned logo.

What shipped

A reorganized website with a new visual language and logo, built to serve students and parents and drive enrollment.
