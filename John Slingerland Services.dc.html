02 — Feature

Helping physicians gain context when time is precious

The pre-visit brief was working as a concept and failing as a page — clinicians were being asked to read where they needed to scan. I ran design experiments, studied how mature products in adjacent industries solve the same problem, and shipped three information-architecture improvements into the alpha build.

03 — AI enabled healthtech

Giving bandwidth to understaffed care teams

An AI copilot for the admissions board used in small clinics by teams needing to coordinate care for recently hospitalized patients. It reads hospital feeds, faxes and call notes, prepares the follow-up work, and lets each team choose how much the AI does on its own.

See the case study

03 — Enterprise UX

We lost real money because our software was too hard to turn on

Installing AppDynamics agents took hundreds of steps and a company-wide naming scheme invented up front. An engineer reframed it for me as a grouping problem the software could solve itself. We shipped it as two commands and three steps.

See the case study

04 — Enterprise UX

Fifteen screens and a box of ticks to grant one person access

Rigid role-based permissions were costing AppDynamics admins time and creating security risk — and nobody could see what they’d actually granted. We replaced ticking boxes with writing rules: an entity-based access model with attributes, tagging and inheritance, validated through research, prototyping and iterative testing.

Process

How a project runs

DESIGN TO FIT YOUR NEEDS. BUILT TO SCALE.

  1. 01

    START WITH A CALL

    Let's talk about what you need over a 90-minute discovery call. We'll cover positioning, messaging, personality, and visual direction.

  2. 02

    IRON OUT THE DETAILS

    This is where we talk strategy. We'll share directions with moodboards, color, typography, architecture, and flows. We make decisions, and we know it'll work.

  3. 03

    DESIGN IT

    We'll bring the strategy to life visually with prototypes, and drafts. We'll get feedback from real users, refined until the structure holds and the visual language is set.

  4. 04

    BRING TO LIFE

    This is where we'll execute on everything we've decided, and deliver it. We'll Package up the project, or kick off the build.

  5. 05

    SHARE WITH THE WORLD

    All deliverables completed: website, app, print materials, and promotional assets. Brand guidelines and organized Figma files delivered.

FAQ

Before you ask

Who do you work with?+

Startups through enterprise teams. Web and brand projects also suit small businesses, creators and service providers.

Can I hire you for just one service?+

Yes. UX architecture, brand, strategy, web and app work are each available on their own or combined in one engagement.

What happens after I get in touch?+

We book a 30-minute call to talk through goals, constraints and timeline. I follow with a written scope and quote. Once you approve it, we set a kickoff date and I share the first deliverable on the schedule we agree.

How is pricing set?+

Projects are fixed-fee, quoted after the discovery call. The scope lists deliverables and revision rounds, so the price does not change as we work. Ongoing support is billed monthly.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on scope. A focused sprint, a brand identity and a multi-page website each run on different timelines, and I give a delivery schedule with every quote. Tightly scoped projects can run in a few days, and bigger projects can take weeks depending on your specific outcomes.

Can you work with our existing design and engineering team?+

Yes. I often work embedded with in-house teams, setting architecture and standards they carry forward. I'm based in the San Francisco Bay Area and work remotely.

Do you build, or only design?+

Both. Web projects include front-end development and CMS setup, so you can edit the site yourself. App projects can run through MVP build, or end with production-ready files and a clean handoff to your engineers.

Do I own the work?+

Yes. You receive the working files, exports and brand assets in the formats you need, whether we continue together or not.

Do you use AI?+

Yes, as a tool, with intention. Specifically to spin up interactive prototypes fast, explore directions faster and handle repetitive early-stage work. Strategy, decisions and final deliverables are crafted, reviewed and refined by hand following a design process I've honed over 20 years.

Do you offer support after launch?+

Yes. After launch I can stay on for optional ongoing support on a monthly retainer, covering updates, improvements and new requests.
