ONE TEAM, ONE PACKAGE: IDENTITY, AND WEBSITE

ProcessThe brand sets the palette and the brick metaphor, the site explains the model to two audiences, the small brand and the brick and mortar shop.

  1. 01

    Brand identity

    A wordmark, a brick-red and charcoal palette and a Montserrat type system.

  2. 02

    WEBSITE 

    A bento homepage with a brand and shop toggle, how it works, the deal for each side and a shop finder.

Website concept

The homepage is a grid of rounded tiles separated by 8px joints, like coursing in a brick wall. The hero switches between online brands and shop owners with one toggle.

Below the grid, plain sections explain how it works in three steps, what each side gets, how the shelf card works, and where to find a shop. A short form requests a match by neighborhood.

StrategyTwo audiences need different reasons to join. Brands want a storefront without a lease. Shops want new inventory and income without the risk, this provides a path to both.

Brand identity

Brick red and charcoal, set in Montserrat. The red comes from the wall, the charcoal from the mortar, and the light grays from the sidewalk in front of the shop.

The mark and the layout share one idea: small, solid units that fit together with even joints. The same rounded tiles appear on the site, the shelf card and the display unit.

StrategyTwo audiences see the brand in different places, a web page and a shop counter. A simple palette and one typeface hold up in both.
