STRATEGY BUILT ON THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

ProcessWe started by understanding the relationship between ABI and their customers, and building on what they valued most. 

  1. 01

    User research

    Phone interviews with users across payroll, management and operations, summarized and presented to the client.

  2. 02

    Website redesign

    A responsive one-page site with paths by venue type, a module explorer, testimonials, stories and a demo request.

  3. 03

    Icon system

    A custom icon set for the product areas, used in the side rail and the full-screen menu.

The starting point

The redesign started with questions: how ABI MasterMind fits into people’s daily work, how clients first heard about it, and how to use the brand position to reach new customers while making the site more useful for existing ones.

The answer was loyalty. So the site leans on it: content that is easy to share, ways to explore by venue and module, and customers speaking for the product.

HOW WE KNOW IT WORKS

Before launch, it gets tested it with the people the research came from and track a few numbers after.
