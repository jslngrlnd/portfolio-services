Greenstack is a mobile iPhone app that provides a simple way to save people money, by taking advantage of membership perks, combining coupons, and providing savings in places they spend the most.

Brand identity

The mark is two offset panels, one deal stacked on another, set beside a rounded wordmark in two greens.

Each deal type gets its own color, so a stack reads at a glance in the app, on the website and in ads.

StrategyGive the behavior a name. “Stacking” became the verb the brand teaches: the mark shows one deal on another, and the deal colors make a stack readable before any copy is read.

The app

Built around the stack meter. Stacking is combining multiple deals and offers to get the most savings.

StrategyMake the saving the hero. Every screen answers one question, “how much am I saving here?”, so the stack meter repeats across the app.

The marketing website

The launch site opens on a stack of deal cards that fans out as you scroll, then walks through what stacking is, the app, and where it works.

Keep scrolling to move through the live site, from desktop down to phone.

StrategyTeach before selling. Stacking is an unfamiliar idea, so the site shows a stack forming before it asks for a download.
